Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), an Institution of Eminence Deemed to be University, has strengthened its standing in the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings 2027, moving to the 551-600 global band from the 601-800 band.

MAHE has also improved its national ranking, moving up to 7th from 11th among Indian institutions in the latest rankings. The improvement comes despite an increase in the number of participating Indian institutions, from 128 in 2026 to 143 in 2027.

MAHE has recorded improvements across the Teaching, Research Environment, Research Quality, International Outlook and Industry pillars. Notably, the Research Quality score rose from 56.4 in 2026 to 62.0 in 2027, underscoring the University’s continued focus on strengthening the quality, impact and visibility of its research.

MAHE’s improved position in the 2027 edition reflects its continued efforts to strengthen its academic and research performance across these indicators.

The MAHE Nature Conference on “Meeting the Challenge of Infectious Diseases in a Changing World” was inaugurated in Manipal, Karnataka, on October 14, 2026. Hosted by the Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), an Institution of Eminence Deemed to be University, and held for the first time in India, the three-day conference is organised in partnership with Nature Medicine, Nature Microbiology, Nature Health and Nature Communications, and will continue until October 16.

The conference brings together researchers, clinicians, public health experts, policymakers, and industry representatives from 20 countries to share scientific findings, examine emerging research, and strengthen collaboration on the prevention and control of infectious diseases.

The conference addresses critical global health priorities, including emerging and re-emerging pathogens, antimicrobial resistance (AMR), pandemic preparedness, disease surveillance, diagnostics, therapeutics, vaccination strategies, and the One Health approach.

#195 in Asia. 11 places higher. A journey of continued progress.

MAHE has been ranked #195 in the QS World University Rankings: Asia 2027, advancing 11 places from #206 last year. With an overall score of 55.3, up from 50.7, this progress reflects our continued commitment to academic excellence, research, global engagement and impact.

MAHE also stands 21st among 328 Indian institutions evaluated this year, improving by one position from the previous edition.

As the higher education landscape becomes increasingly competitive, we remain committed to strengthening our global presence and creating meaningful impact through education and research.
